One line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week: a hurried, boringly specific excuse about the council collecting a broken fridge and fining you if nobody is home to sign for it — the kind of dull municipal errand a tired manager has heard a hundred times and can hardly argue with, which is exactly why it almost works.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network) writes the single-line excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`), after checking it against the gate: 1–140 characters, at most 20 words, one line.
